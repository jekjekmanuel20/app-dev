# Vinland Saga

## Overview
**Vinland Saga** is a Japanese historical anime and manga series written and illustrated by *Makoto Yukimura*.  
It’s set during the **Viking era** and follows the story of **Thorfinn**, a young warrior seeking revenge and purpose.

---

## Main Characters
- **Thorfinn** – The main protagonist, driven by revenge.
- **Askeladd** – A cunning Viking leader with a mysterious past.
- **Canute** – A timid prince who undergoes a powerful transformation.
- **Thors** – Thorfinn’s father, known as *Thors the Troll*, a legendary warrior.

---

## Themes
1. **Revenge vs. Forgiveness**
2. **The True Meaning of a Warrior**
3. **Freedom and Peace**
4. **The Brutality of War**

---

### Why I Love It
Vinland Saga combines **historical depth**, **intense action**, and **emotional storytelling**.  
The animation quality, especially during battles, is top-tier, and the character development is profound.
